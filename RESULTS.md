# Results — synthetic evaluation

**Threshold:** `DECISION_THRESHOLD = 2.0`, fixed before the evaluation run and never fitted
to test data.
**Data:** 4 input styles × 8 simulated people × (8 enrolment + 3 fresh + 3 tired +
3 stranger + 3 mimic) = 640 sessions, seed `20261102`, generated by `marrow.py`.
**Scoring:** mean of the two worst weighted robust z-scores against the enrolment profile;
accept if `score <= 2.0`.

| Style | Genuine | False doubt (FRR) | Impostors | Admitted (FAR) | Swap trials | Swap admitted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mouse + keyboard | 48 | 3 (6.2%) | 48 | 0 (0.0%) | 48 | 0 |
| slow pointer + dictation | 48 | 1 (2.1%) | 48 | 0 (0.0%) | 48 | 0 |
| eye gaze + OSK | 48 | 0 (0.0%) | 48 | 1 (2.1%) | 48 | 0 |
| switch scanning | 48 | 0 (0.0%) | 48 | 6 (12.5%) | 48 | 0 |
| **All** | **192** | **4 (2.1%)** | **192** | **7 (3.6%)** | **192** | **0 (0.0%)** |

Raw: `out/demo_results.json`. Reproduce: `python3 marrow.py demo`.

## What the swap test shows

Each subject's genuine sessions were scored against the enrolment profile of the **next**
subject. 0 of 192 were accepted. The method's distance is driven by low-level dynamics,
so a profile of another person does not open the vault.

## What the numbers do not show

- **These are synthetic people, deliberately separated.** A real population is closer;
  expect worse separation and a need for per-user calibration.
- **Switch scanning is the weak cell.** With no pointer, only text timing, correction and
  source remain; 6 of 48 impostors (mimics) were admitted. That is a real limit of the
  current feature set, not a rounding artefact.
- **Mimics copy the surface.** They copy movement pace and typing rhythm but keep their
  own correction habit; a mimic who also copied correction behaviour would be harder.
- No real-user session, no browser capture, no replay or takeover defence has been
  evaluated.

## Design choices that matter

1. **Ratios, not raw timings.** Settle-to-click is divided by movement duration, so
   fatigue and slower devices shift both numerator and denominator.
2. **Duration down-weighted.** A tired owner is slower; treating slowness as imposture
   produced the first version's high false-doubt rate.
3. **Worst-two aggregation.** A weighted mean diluted one decisive mismatch (typing
   rhythm) and admitted wrong profiles; the mean of the two worst z-scores fixed it
   without turning ordinary variation into doubt.
4. **Spread floors.** Five enrolment sessions underestimate session-to-session variation;
   per-feature floors stop ordinary variation being treated as evidence.
